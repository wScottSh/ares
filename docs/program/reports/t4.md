## DEVIATIONS
1. Time is absolute in T4 (plan title, sketch clock.hpp). The relative nall queue and the CPU-driven synchronize stay for T5. syncClock carries the old relative bookkeeping exactly.
2. clock.hpp adds UnitsPerSecond and us/ms/seconds. VclkAccumulator takes a Ratio period, which keeps PAL exact, and normalizes so it never overflows. Ratio moved out of the generated header.
3. New rows: rsp.slot (1 rclk, wiki via clocks.md) replaces legacy.rsp.issue and legacy.rsp.stall. legacy.rdp.step-quantum (1 s) is the T3 follow-up.
4. Restated rows, same times as before: pi.write-busy 200 pclk, rsp.halted-quantum 64 pclk, pif.step-quantum 40960 pclk, cart.rtc-tick 1 s.
5. nall priority_queue is 64-bit. Without this, the 4x resolution would shrink the 32-bit range to 2.86 s, below the 64DD sleep delay.
6. n64-run rsp_busy_clocks stays in 187.5 MHz ticks so results compare across T4. The RSP profile and emux XPROFREAD 0x200/0x201 now count units.
7. The AI's power-on 44100 Hz becomes a whole VCLK divider (1103 VCLKs, 44,136.7 Hz).
8. The VI change was not in the plan's expected diff. The plan names vi.cpp on VclkAccumulator, and the exact VCLK moves MM timing from field 25. Proven by the diagnostic build above.
9. The thar0 checks.tsv fix (commit aa0350a89) is outside T4's files. It was needed because the tools merge broke the build. The zcmp and fill-mode mappings are my choice.
10. Commit trailers use the session's attribution line (Claude Opus 5.5), not the brief's.
